About our suspicious little shop

Welcome to Illegally Obtained F1 Parts, the fictional home of the paddock's missing inventory. The name is a joke; this is a student catalogue, not a real parts business.

Our shelves bring together five examples of F1 engineering: a DRS actuator, a Brabham fan assembly, a Ferrari F2002 chassis, McLaren's extra brake pedal and Ferrari's 2026 Macarena rear wing.

Some of these ideas helped cars find more grip. Others helped drivers turn or reduced drag on the straights. Together they show how inventive Formula 1 engineering can be.
