About this part

The Ferrari F2002 used a carbon-fibre and honeycomb composite chassis. The chassis is the structural core around which the cockpit and the rest of the car are arranged.

This catalogue entry focuses on the chassis. The gallery shows complete F2002 cars for context; the chassis is not photographed separately.
