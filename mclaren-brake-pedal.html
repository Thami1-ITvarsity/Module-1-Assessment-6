About this part

McLaren's 1997 brake-steer system used an extra pedal to apply a brake at one rear wheel. This helped the driver rotate the car through a corner.

The extra control gave David Coulthard a four-pedal cockpit. Our main picture shows the pedal arrangement; the other photographs show the correct MP4/12 car for context.
